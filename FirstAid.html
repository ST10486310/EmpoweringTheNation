First Aid Training

Essential life-saving skills for emergencies

Course Overview

Fees: R1500

Purpose

To provide first aid awareness and basic life support to individuals, enabling them to respond effectively in emergency situations.

Course Content

  • Wounds and bleeding - Learn how to manage different types of wounds and control bleeding
  • Burns and fractures - Proper treatment for burns and immobilization techniques for fractures
  • Emergency scene management - How to assess and manage emergency situations safely
  • Cardio-Pulmonary Resuscitation (CPR) - Life-saving techniques for cardiac emergencies
  • Respiratory distress - Management of choking, blocked airways, and breathing difficulties

This hands-on course provides practical skills that can save lives in critical situations. Our certified instructors will guide you through realistic scenarios to build your confidence in emergency response.

Course Benefits

  • Certified first aid training
  • Hands-on practical sessions
  • Small class sizes for personalized attention
  • Certificate of completion
  • Skills applicable in workplace and home
  • Meets industry safety standards

Who Should Attend

This course is ideal for workplace first aiders, parents, teachers, community workers, and anyone interested in learning life-saving skills.
